Lossless transfer. Text splits into ordered chunks. Scan strictly in sequence — the corporate scanner concatenates each decoded chunk into your target field, reproducing the original. Lower bytes/QR if the scanner struggles with dense codes; raise error correction (H) for scratched or low-quality scanning at the cost of capacity. Note: this works losslessly only if the scanner adds no suffix (e.g. Enter) between scans — if it does, those characters land between chunks.
